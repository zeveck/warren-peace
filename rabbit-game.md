# A rabbit adventure-RPG with an army-building war (title: yours to choose)

2D isometric pixel art. A large game. Come up with a strong title: warm
rather than harsh. Ideas I like: "Warren Peace" (a war between warrens
that ends in peace), "Hare-Raising", "Burrowed Time". Use one, or better.

I'm sketching the idea; I'm not wed to the particulars. Anything marked
"maybe", and anything not covered here (the story's details, characters,
dialog, allies, items), is yours to invent. Make it a full game with a real
story, plenty of dialog, and a party you care about.

## The feel
- Cozy-epic and all-ages, with a Lovecraft thread that's genuinely eerie but
  never gory or hopeless.
- Inspired by the mood of Watership Down (rabbits, the downs, a warren under
  threat), but all our own characters, names and story.

## The start
- You're a rabbit on the downs: hop around, talk to other rabbits, nibble
  grass.
- Your warren below is a 2D maze of tunnels and rooms, with rabbits to meet
  there too.
- A few minutes in, the world shakes: rival rabbits attack the warren, wreck
  it and carry some of your rabbits off. Destruction and missing friends,
  not gore.
- On the downs you can start an RTS battle against the invaders at any
  time, but they have an army and you're alone, so it can't be won yet.
- The elder gives you the key to The Depths, a door deep in the warren that
  never opened before, and tells you to find allies below.

## The Depths and the worlds
- The deeper warren has enemies and relatively easy RPG battles.
- Every warren has its own sealed Depths. Each one leads to a story world,
  and to a layer of the Lovecraft depths, which run beneath everything and
  get deeper and eerier with each warren.
- Your home warren: The Wind in the Willows and Alice in Wonderland, and the
  shallows of the Lovecraft depths (carvings, whispers, a hint of what's
  below).
- Each world has warrens to explore and a surface to wander, with locals to
  meet and recruit, including a key ally or two per place (and a rabbit
  friend back home in your warren).
- Allies, for example: moles and badgers, birds, caterpillars, playing
  cards, winged monkeys, unspeakable horrors. The Cheshire Cat can join
  your party.
- All the worlds come from public-domain stories: use the original books,
  not later films.

## Battles, party and army
- RPG battles: turn-based, you plus up to 2 party members (a party of 3),
  chosen from your recruits.
- Everyone who joins you joins your army, and is available in the RTS
  battles.
- A light RTS: strategy over fast clicking.
- Shops: buy armor and gear to outfit both your RPG party and your army.

## The war, and why
- Long ago a warren dug too deep and woke something ancient beneath the
  Lovecraft depths; that's why the elder's door stayed locked. Now the
  invaders' warren has dug too deep too: it's got into their minds, and it
  spreads from warren to warren. The invaders aren't villains; they're its
  first victims.
- Win the RTS battle on the downs with the allies from your home warren's
  worlds: take your warren back and rescue your rabbits.
- Then free the warrens it has taken, one at a time. Each one freed joins
  your army and opens its own world and a deeper layer of the depths:
  - the first: the Moon Rabbit;
  - the second: Oz (the book's, not the film's);
  - last, the invaders' own warren: Journey to the Center of the Earth, the
    hole they dug, leading down to the source.
- The finale: the deepest depths, with every freed warren behind you.
- The ending: all the warrens freed and at peace, together on the downs.
- (What the creature is, how the influence works and how it's broken are
  yours to invent.)
