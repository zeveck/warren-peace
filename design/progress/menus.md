7. **Shop icons** (`screens/shop.js`, new `drawIconFit` in gfx.js): rows are 24 px (9 per page), every icon is fitted to a 21 px box centred in its row (16 px army icons scale up, 24 px gear icons down), text and faces re-centred. Same size on every tab, inside the highlight.
8. **Camera** (`world/explore.js` `camTarget`): the old safe box (110 px from the sides, feet 100 px from the top) is now a comfort box round the centre: his body stays within 64 px of the centre across and 26 px up or down, and it still wins over the map clamp, so at a map's edge the view shows a little void instead of pinning him to the screen edge. Dead zone (40x22) unchanged inside it.
9. **Fast clicks**: the click tests used the pointer's *current* position at draw time. Playwright (and a fast player) can press, release and already move the pointer on within one frame, so the hit-test missed the button (and the explore update walked to the new spot instead). Also dialogue choices needed a hover frame: `this.hover` was computed in the previous draw, so a click whose move arrived in the same frame was ignored or picked the old hovered choice.
   Fix: `input.mouse.px/py` record where the left press went down; `input.clickIn(x,y,w,h)` / gfx `clickIn` test against it. `button`, camp `rowSel`, shop rows and the explore tile click (`clickAt(px, py)`, `overHud(px, py)`) use it; `world/script.js` dialogue choices compute the clicked choice from the press point and the last drawn layout (`choiceGeo`), no hover needed.
